What Is Red Light Therapy?

Every day, your body runs on cellular energy. But as you age, stress, inflammation, and poor sleep slow that energy production down. That’s when recovery takes longer, wrinkles appear, and focus starts to fade.

Using targeted wavelengths of red and near-infrared (NIR) light, it penetrates deep into tissues to stimulate the mitochondria, the “power plants” of your cells.

The result?

  • Support energy (ATP) production for faster recovery and peak performance
  • Help manage everyday inflammation to soothe common aches and stiffness
  • Promote smoother, firmer skin by supporting collagen production
  • Support sharper focus and clarity so your mind keeps up with your ambitions
  • Encourages deeper sleep and better resilience for the day ahead

Got Questions? We’ve Got You Covered.

How is this different from other red light panels?

Most panels give you 2–3 wavelengths and only one adjustment level. RLX delivers six clinical wavelengths with per-channel control (0–100%) and keeps high output at a comfortable 6-12", so sessions are faster and easier. It’s flicker-free, measures 0.00 µT EMF at 6", premium all-metal housing, and backed by an industry leading 4-year warranty.

Do I need to be naked for it to work?

No. Light passes through thin, light fabrics. For skin-focused goals, direct skin exposure is best. For recovery/sleep/stress goals, normal at-home wear is fine—just expose the area you’re targeting (e.g., quads, back, neck).

Is it safe for daily use?

Yes. RLX is designed for daily, at-home use: flicker-free, zero-detect EMF at 6", and FDA Class II device registration with core safety certifications (FCC, CE, RoHS). As with any light therapy, avoid staring directly into LEDs and follow the timing guidance below.

How long is each session?

Because RLX keeps power high at real distance, sessions are short:

  • Face / skin: 5–8 min at ~8–12"
  • Muscle & joint recovery: 6–10 min per area at ~8–14"
  • Sleep / stress (evening chest/neck exposure): 5–8 min at ~10–14"

Start 3–5 days/week. If you’re new, begin at the short end and increase gradually. (You can save presets in the app.)

Please note: Everyone’s body is different. We recommend consulting with your doctor or healthcare provider to determine the session duration and frequency that is best for your specific needs.

What results can I expect — and when?

Timelines vary, but common patterns:

  • Post-workout soreness / joint comfort: many feel changes in 1–2 weeks.
  • Sleep quality / stress balance: often improves within 1–3 weeks of evening use.
  • Skin tone / texture: visible changes typically build over 4–8 weeks of steady use.

Consistency beats intensity. Short, comfortable sessions done regularly win.

Is there a warranty?

Yes. 4-year warranty.

Will this help with testosterone or hormones?

Research suggests red/near-infrared light can support better sleep quality, reduce perceived stress, and aid recovery—all factors that indirectly support healthy hormones. While some early studies explore direct hormone effects, the evidence is still developing.

Can more than one person use it?

Absolutely. Multiple users can share the panel. You can keep routines simple with the per-channel presets, or save different presets per user in the app.
