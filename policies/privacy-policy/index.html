Privacy Policy — Paradiseee Wellness

Last Updated: October 23, 2025

Entity: Paradiseee Wellness

Contact: support@paradiseeewellness.com

Mailing Address: Available upon request.

1) Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Paradiseee Wellness (“we,” “us,” “our”) collects, uses, and shares personal information when you visit our website, make a purchase, contact support, or otherwise interact with us online. By using our site, you agree to this Policy.

2) What We Collect

Information you provide • Orders & Support: name, email, phone, billing/shipping addresses, order details, messages to support.

• Quiz / Forms: multiple-choice answers about interests/product fit and your email if you opt in. We do not request medical histories or other sensitive health data via the site.

• Newsletter/Marketing Opt-ins: email (no SMS at this time).

Information collected automatically Device/browser type, IP address, pages viewed, time on site, and similar diagnostics collected by our commerce platform to operate the store and prevent fraud.

No advertising pixels are active at this time. If we later enable analytics/ads pixels (e.g., Google Analytics 4, Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel), we will update this Policy and our cookie banner accordingly.

3) How We Use Information

• Fulfill orders, process payments, arrange shipping/returns, and provide customer support.

• Operate, secure, and improve our website and services (fraud prevention, debugging, analytics).

• Send transactional emails (order/shipping updates) and—with your consent—marketing emails (unsubscribe any time).

• Comply with legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

4) Legal Bases (where GDPR/UK law applies)

If applicable, we process personal data under: contract (fulfilling your order), legitimate interests (security, improvement), consent (marketing/cookies), and legal obligation (tax/audit).

5) How We Share Information We share personal information with service providers under contracts limiting use to our instructions:

• E-commerce & Payments: our store platform and payment processors (e.g., Shopify Payments).

• Fulfillment & Logistics: shipping/fulfillment partners to deliver orders.

• Email Marketing: providers we use to send opted-in communications (email only).

• Security/Fraud & Professional Services: tools and advisors (e.g., legal, accounting) when needed.

We may also share information to comply with law, enforce our terms, or protect rights, safety, and property.

We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. If this changes, we will add a “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” link and update this Policy.

6) Cookies & Similar Technologies

Our site uses essential cookies required for core functions (e.g., cart, checkout). We currently do not use analytics or advertising cookies. If we enable analytics/ad pixels in the future, we will update this Policy and display a cookie banner (and obtain consent where required) and provide controls to manage preferences.

7) Marketing Communications

With your consent, we may send marketing emails. You can unsubscribe using the link in any marketing email or by contacting support@paradiseeewellness.com. Transactional emails related to your orders will still be sent as needed.

8) Children’s Privacy

Our site is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9) Data Retention

• Orders/Transactions: retained as required for tax, accounting, and legal obligations (commonly up to 7 years).

• Marketing Data: retained until you unsubscribe or we remove inactive contacts.

• Support Records: retained as needed to manage your account/our relationship, then securely deleted or anonymized.

10) Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures (e.g., encryption in transit, access controls). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

11) International Data Transfers

We are a U.S. company. Your information may be stored and processed in the United States and in other countries where our service providers operate (e.g., cloud, payments, logistics). Where required, we use appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses).

12) Your Privacy Rights Depending on your location, you may have rights to access or download your data, correct inaccuracies, delete your data (subject to legal exceptions), object to or restrict certain processing, withdraw consent (for marketing/cookies where applicable), and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (EU/UK, if applicable).

To make a request, email support@paradiseeewellness.com. We will verify your identity and respond within the time required by law.

California (CPRA): We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. If this changes, we will provide a compliant opt-out mechanism and update this Policy.

13) Third-Party Links

Our site may link to third-party websites or social media. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

14) Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we post changes, we update the “Last Updated” date above. Material changes will be highlighted on the site.

15) Contact Us

Questions or requests about this Policy? Email: support@paradiseeewellness.com. Mailing Address: Available upon request.
