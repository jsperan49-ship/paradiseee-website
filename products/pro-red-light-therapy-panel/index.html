What Makes This Different From Other Red Light Panels?

The RLX 348 PRO delivers 6 clinically studied wavelengths (630, 660, 810, 830, 850, 1060 nm) with rare per-channel control (0-100%). Most panels only offer 2-3 wavelengths with a single on/off switch. Our panel also features ~170 mW/cm² irradiance, meaning faster sessions and better results.

How Long Is Each Session?

Most users see optimal results with just 10-15 minutes per session at 6 inches from the panel. The high irradiance means you get your therapeutic dose faster than lower-powered devices that require 20-30 minutes.

Is It Safe For Daily Use?

Yes. Red and near-infrared light therapy is non-invasive and has been extensively studied with no significant adverse effects reported. The RLX 348 PRO is manufactured in an FDA-registered facility, flicker-free, and measures 0.00 µT EMF at 6 inches. It's designed for daily use.

What Results Can I Expect, And When?

Results vary by goal. Published research shows sleep and energy improvements within 1-2 weeks of consistent use. Skin and pain relief changes are typically measured at the 2-4 week mark in clinical studies. Recovery benefits may be noticeable after just a few sessions.
